Hey fam! let's get some fries and chicken!

Ingredients

For the fries;

  • Potatoes
  • Oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Peel and cut potatoes into thin strips.
  2. Wash and dry them.
  3. Heat oil and fry until golden and crispy.
  4. Add salt.

Ingredients

For the chicken;

  • Chicken pieces
  • salt
  • pepper
  • garlic
  • seasonings
  • oil

Steps

  1. Wash and season the chicken with salt, pepper, garlic, and seasoning.
  2. Heat a little oil.
  3. Fry the chicken until brown and cooked through.
  4. Serve with the fries and ketchup (optional).

Enjoy!!
